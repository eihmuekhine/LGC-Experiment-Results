1. Reason. Latent-HSJA is the closest decision-based attack with a generative latent prior. A re-run of Latent-HSJA
   and HSJA on our pairs removes three limits of a comparison with the published numbers alone: (a) the published SIM
   preprocessing is not reproducible from the release (256 x 256 inputs to IR_101(input_size = 112)); (b) Latent-HSJA
   is published against its reconstruction G(w_src), not the real source; (c) the published gender pairs may contain
   same-label pairs (the released notebook applies no y_src != y_trg filter; no gender attack notebook is released).
